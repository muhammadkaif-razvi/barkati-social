## Budget (₹100/day)
- India mein Meta ad spend par GST (18%) alag se lagta hai (aapke purane plan mein bhi: ₹3,000 incl. GST = ₹84/day). Billing page par ek baar confirm kar lijiye.
  - Kul kharcha ₹100/day chahiye → daily budget **₹85** rakhiye (≈ ₹100 with GST, ≈ ₹3,000/month).
  - Budget ₹100 rakhenge → ≈ ₹118/day charge hoga (≈ ₹3,540/month).
- 3 ads ek hi ad set mein, ek hi budget. Meta khud jo ad behtar chale usko zyada dikhata hai.

## Pehle ye ready rakhiye (one-time)
1. Facebook Page "Barkati" aur Instagram @barkati_in Business Suite mein connected.
2. WhatsApp number Page se linked: Page → Settings → Linked accounts → WhatsApp → number + OTP. (WhatsApp Business app wala number best hai.)
3. Payment method: Business Suite → Settings → Billing & payments → UPI / card add.
4. WhatsApp Business app mein greeting message + quick reply "DEMO" ready rakhiye (jaise: "Namaste! Aapka business ka naam, city aur kaam batayiye. Hum aapka homepage demo free banake WhatsApp pe bhejenge.").

## Setup — Ads Manager (recommended: teeno videos ek saath test hote hain)
Business Suite → left menu **All tools → Ads Manager** → **+ Create**.
1. **Campaign objective:** Engagement. Campaign name: `BARKATI | CTWA | Hyd | Nov26`.
   - Advantage campaign budget: OFF (budget ad set level pe).
2. **Ad set** (name `Hyd 25km | 25-60 | Advantage+`):
   - Conversion location: **Messaging apps → WhatsApp** (number select karein). Performance goal: *Maximise number of conversations*.
   - Daily budget: **₹85** (ya ₹100, upar dekhiye). Schedule: aaj se 30 din (end date set kar dijiye).
   - Location: **Hyderabad + 25 km** (people living in or recently in). Age: **25–60**. Gender: all. Language: blank.
   - Audience: Advantage+ audience ON. Suggestion (optional): interests "Small business", "Entrepreneurship", "Small and medium enterprises", "Business owner pages / Facebook Page admins".
   - Placements: **Advantage+ placements** (ya manual: Instagram Reels, Instagram Stories, Facebook Reels, Facebook Stories, Feed).
3. **Ad 1** (name `A1 Problem+data`):
   - Identity: Facebook Page Barkati + Instagram @barkati_in.
   - Format: Single image or video → upload **barkati-ad-1.mp4**. Thumbnail: first frame (hook already visible).
   - Primary text:
     > Customer pehle Google karta hai, phir call. Website nahi hai to call doosre business ko jaata hai.
     > BARKATI aapke business ki website banata hai: rates, photos, map, WhatsApp + call button.
     > Pehle free homepage demo dekhiye, pasand aaye tabhi payment.
     > Source: IAMAI report, Jan 2026
   - Headline: `Free homepage demo, pehle dekhiye` · Call to action: **Send WhatsApp message**.
   - Message template (WhatsApp greeting / pre-filled message): customer ki taraf se **"DEMO"**; aapka greeting upar wala.
4. Ad set ke andar **Duplicate** karke Ad 2 aur Ad 3 banaiye, sirf video + text badliye:
   - **A2 Offer** (barkati-ad-2.mp4)
     > Pehle website dekhiye, phir payment.
     > WhatsApp pe DEMO bhejiye, hum aapka homepage free banake dikhayenge.
     > Plans from ₹4,999 · one-time · no monthly fee · domain aapke naam.
     Headline: `Plans from ₹4,999, no monthly fee`
   - **A3 Sample** (barkati-ad-3.mp4)
     > Hall, caterer, showroom ya packers: aapki website aisi dikh sakti hai.
     > Rates, photos, map aur Call + WhatsApp, sab pehli screen par.
     > Video mein sample design hai (client nahi). Apna free demo WhatsApp pe maangiye.
     Headline: `Apne business ka free demo`
5. **Publish.** Review mein usually kuch ghante lagte hain.

## Short route — Business Suite "Create ad" (agar Ads Manager mushkil lage)
Business Suite → **Ads → Create ad** → Goal **Get more messages** → WhatsApp → video upload (ek video per ad) → text + CTA "Send WhatsApp message" → Audience: Hyderabad +25 km, 25–60 → Placements auto → Budget ₹85/day, 30 din → Publish. Isme 3 alag ads banane padte hain (har ek ka apna budget), isliye ₹85 ko teen mein mat baantiye: pehle A1 7 din chalaiye, phir A2, phir A3; ya Ads Manager wala setup use kijiye.

## Chalne ke baad (har din 2 minute)
- Day 1–3: kuch mat badaliye (learning phase).
- Dekhne wala number: **Cost per messaging conversation started** + kitne log ne sach mein DEMO bheja / details di.
- Day 5–7: jis ad ki cost per conversation sabse zyada aur conversations sabse kam ho, use **pause**. Baaki do chalne dijiye.
- Har WhatsApp message ka jawab jaldi dijiye; slow reply se ad ka paisa bekaar jaata hai.
- Kisi ko result ka promise mat kijiye (views, ranking, orders).
- 14 din baad: winner ad ka naya version (same angle, naya hook) bana ke test kariye.
