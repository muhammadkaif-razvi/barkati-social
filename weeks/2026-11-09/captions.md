## Mon 9 Nov · 10:30 IST · post · `post-mon.png`
**Write your About us in 4 short lines** (tip, no selling)

Your About us can say more in 4 lines than most say in a full page.

1. What you do, in plain words.
2. Where you serve: your area, your city, nearby towns.
3. One real detail: since when, or who runs it. Only if it's true.
4. Skip words like "best quality". A customer can't check them, so they skip them.

Short and true builds more trust than long and fancy.

Save this for the day you write yours.

## Wed 11 Nov · 10:30 IST · post · `post-wed.png`
**"My nephew can make it."** (objection O7: agree → reframe → one check)

"My nephew can make it." Fair enough, and we mean that.

When it's your business, trust matters most. Someone you know is a good place to start.

Whoever builds your website, check three basics:
• The domain is registered in your name
• It opens fast on a phone
• There is an enquiry or WhatsApp button

And ask one question before work starts: "If I need a change later, who will do it?"
