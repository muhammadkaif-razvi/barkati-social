## Mon 23 Nov · 10:30 IST · post · `post-mon.png`
**Name your services the way customers search** (tip, no selling)

Customers search in their own words. Your website should use the same ones.

1. Use your customers' words: "wedding catering", not "F&B solutions".
2. One service, one clear line: "Birthday cakes, made to order."
3. Add the area you serve. People often type the area name in their search.
4. Put a real photo of your own work next to each service.

Plain words help a customer know in seconds that you do what they need.

Save this for the day you write your services page.

## Wed 25 Nov · 10:30 IST · post · `post-wed.png`
**"We had a website. Nothing happened."** (objection O6: agree → reframe → one check)

"We had a website. Nothing happened." We hear this, and it's a fair thing to feel.

Many websites really don't bring enquiries. Usually it comes down to one of three things:
→ it opens slowly on the phone
→ there's no WhatsApp or call button
→ the rates, timings or photos are old

One check for today: open your old site on your phone, on mobile data. Is it quick? Can you see the button without scrolling? Is the info current?
