## Mon 26 Oct · 10:30 · post-mon.png — 4 photo tips for your website

Good photos do half the work on a business website.

1. Use real photos of your shop or your work, not stock images.
2. Shoot in daylight with the phone held straight, and clean the lens first.
3. Add one line under each photo: what it is, and where.
4. Keep image files small so the page opens quickly on the phone.

Save this for your next photo day.
